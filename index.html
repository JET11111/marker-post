About

Marker Post shows the nearest recorded marker post to your position. Distances are straight-line distances to recorded points, not surveyed measurements. Check the carriageway before you call it in.

Unofficial personal tool. Not affiliated with, or endorsed by, National Highways.

Mount the phone and read it at a glance. Do not handle it while driving.

Data and licences

Road network, emergency areas and sign messages: National Highways. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Sources: Network Model, Emergency Areas.

Marker posts and junctions: the South East Region map compilation. Its survey date is unknown and the source cautions that posts may not be aligned. Missing posts are never estimated.

Base map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
